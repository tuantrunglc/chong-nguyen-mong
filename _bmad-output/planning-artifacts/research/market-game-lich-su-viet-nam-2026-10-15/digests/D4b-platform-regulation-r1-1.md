# D4b — Platforms, Vietnamese game market & regulation (round 1)
Accessed: 2026-10-15 for all sources. Class: P = primary/official text or first-party data; S = secondary (press/aggregator reporting primary data); A = analyst/vendor estimate.

## Findings

1. **Claim:** Vietnam's mobile game market reached ~$825M revenue in 2025 (+9.16% YoY), with 54M mobile players; only 7–10% of players pay, 2–3% are high spenders; ARPU ~$15.27. MMORPG, 4X strategy and team battle take most revenue. Domestic payment channels grew strongly.
   Source: https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-50335 (reporting PocketGamer.biz / Gamota "Vietnam Mobile Gaming Year-in-Review 2025"; PocketGamer original https://www.pocketgamer.biz/vietnams-mobile-games-market-reached-825m-in-revenue-in-2025 returned 403). Publisher: WN Hub. Pub: 2026-03-16. Confidence: medium. Class: A (publisher-vendor estimate, reported by S).

2. **Claim:** A different market estimate (GameGeek) put Vietnam's gaming revenue at $315M in 2024 and projected more than $430M for 2025 (+36.4%). It also cited 9.6B downloads in 2024 and more than 3,000 game studios in Vietnam. The figures disagree with #1, probably because they define the market differently (possibly revenue of Vietnamese studios). Read as a range, not a point.
   Source: https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1720441/gaming-industry-eyes-over-430-million-revenue-this-year.html. Publisher: Viet Nam News. Pub: 2025-06-28. Confidence: low-medium (definition unclear; its "37.3% of global game app downloads" claim looks implausible and is unverified). Class: S/A.

3. **Claim:** A third figure cites Vietnam's games market at $1.66B (2025), rising to $2.42B by 2029 (CAGR 9.77%). It says games are a sub-sector of the digital content industry and frames this as a 2025–2030 strategy target. Bloomberg (2023) is cited as saying Vietnam is a top-5 mobile-game-producing country.
   Source: https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1716994/viet-nam-targets-us-2-4-billion-in-gaming-industry-revenue-by-2029.html. Publisher: Viet Nam News. Pub: 2025-05-05. Confidence: low-medium (it is unclear whether this is a market forecast or a policy target). Class: S.
   Note: an earlier search snippet (Statista via 80.lv/Xsolla) gave a split of $472M = $322M PC/console + $150M mobile. **Not fetched, so unverified.** It contradicts #1 and should not be used without checking.

4. **Claim:** Vietnamese is 0.25% of Steam users by language (−0.04 pts) in the September 2026 Steam Hardware & Software Survey. For comparison: English 34.20%, Simplified Chinese 31.39%, Russian 9.05%. This means the Vietnamese-speaking Steam audience is very small, so a Steam title has to target English (and possibly Chinese) players to be viable.
   Source: https://store.steampowered.com/hwsurvey/Steam-Hardware-Software-Survey-Welcome-to-Steam. Publisher: Valve. Pub: Sept 2026 survey. Confidence: high (survey language share is not the same as the player count). Class: P.

5. **Claim:** Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP (effective 2024-12-25) classifies games by type in Art. 37:
   - G1: multiplayer interaction through a server.
   - G2: player-to-server interaction only.
   - G3: multiplayer without server interaction.
   - G4: "trò chơi điện tử được tải về, không có sự tương tác giữa người chơi" (downloaded games with no interaction between players).

   G1 needs a service licence plus a release decision for each game. G2, G3 and G4 need a Certificate of service provision plus a release-confirmation notice. Age ratings (Art. 38) are 00+, 12+, 16+, 18+. **Only enterprises established under Vietnamese law may provide these games. Individuals are not provided for**, and foreign organisations or individuals must set up a Vietnamese enterprise (Art. 37(4), 39, 48). G2–G4 providers need financial, organisational and staffing capacity suited to their scale (Art. 48(1c)).
   Source: https://hethongphapluat.com/decree-no-147-2024-nd-cp-dated-november-9-2024-on-management-provision-and-use-of-internet-services-and-cyber-information/chuong-3/muc-3 (plus https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-147-2024-nd-cp-quan-ly-cung-cap-su-dung-dich-vu-internet-va-thong-tin-tren-mang/chuong-6). Publisher: Hệ thống pháp luật (decree text mirror). Pub: decree 2024-11-09. Confidence: medium-high. The fetch tool paraphrased the text and the last ~18% of the page was not read. Class: P (mirror).
   **Implication (inference, not legal advice):** a premium single-player Steam game sold to players in Vietnam is likely G4. Distributing it to Vietnamese users would formally need a Vietnamese enterprise with a G4 certificate. Whether this applies to sales that go **only** through foreign storefronts, or only to overseas players, is NOT answered by the sources fetched (see Gaps).

6. **Claim:** Licensing is two-tier (company licence plus a per-title licence), which makes it harder than in most countries. Legal service fees run about 30M VND (~$1,200) per application. On average only 1 in 35 games uploaded to Google Play or the App Store becomes profitable, and over 95% fail or are abandoned within 6 months. Market growth is above 20% a year. Vietnam exports games, but domestic players mainly play imported (often Chinese) games. Politburo Resolution 80 promotes games as a cultural export, and the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (Bộ VHTTDL) proposes tax incentives for games with Vietnamese cultural values.
   Source: https://vnexpress.net/xuat-khau-game-viet-5107547.html. Publisher: VnExpress. Pub: 2026-08-11. Confidence: medium (opinion piece; 1-in-35 stat source unclear). Class: S.

7. **Claim:** A draft decree amending Decree 147 was put out for consultation on 2026-07-23. It defines "game Việt" by three criteria:
   - made by a Vietnamese enterprise that holds the IP;
   - contains Vietnamese cultural/historical elements;
   - at least 30% of direct production staff are Vietnamese.

   Proposed incentives:
   - Corporate income tax: 2-year exemption, then 50% off for 4 years.
   - Personal income tax on staff salaries: 2-year exemption, then 50% off for 4 years.
   - 90-day trial launch with 10 days' notice, capped at 10,000 players and no revenue.

   A Bộ VHTTDL expert council would assess cultural/historical content. **Still a draft.**
   Source: https://vneconomy.vn/de-xuat-loat-uu-dai-thue-chua-tung-co-cho-game-viet.htm. Publisher: VnEconomy. Pub: ~2026-07 (consultation date 2026-07-23). Confidence: medium-high that the draft exists; low on its final content. Class: S.
   **Relevance:** a Vietnamese-history game fits the "game Việt" definition directly, but the incentives need an enterprise, not an individual.

8. **Claim:** In May 2024 all Vietnamese ISPs blocked Steam, with no official explanation. The suspected reason was that Steam sells games that have not been licensed in Vietnam, while domestic developers must be licensed. Players could not log in or launch games they had bought.
   Source: https://www.gizchina.com/2024/05/13/steam-banned-in-vietnam/. Publisher: Gizchina. Pub: 2024-05-13. Confidence: medium on the event. Class: S. That access was restored afterwards is an **unverified belief**: it was not confirmed this run, although the September 2026 survey still shows Vietnamese at 0.25%.

9. **Claim (Steam economics):** For 2025 Steam releases (~20,282):
   - The median lifetime revenue is about $1,000.
   - About 49% had fewer than 10 reviews (≈300 copies) and 11% had zero reviews.
   - 2.99% reached 1,000 reviews and about 1.5% grossed $1M or more.
   - The median price of top-selling new games fell from $19.50 (Feb 2023) to $15.64 (Oct 2025).
   Source: https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/median-steam-game-2026. Publisher: Steam Page Analyzer (blog aggregating VG Insights, SteamDB, GameDiscoverCo, Alinea). Pub: 2026-09-04. Confidence: medium. Class: A.
   Corroboration: a search snippet (Gamalytic via Yahoo/Mezha, not fetched) said 65.9% of 2025 releases (8,388 games) earned under $1,000 and 47.4% sold under 100 copies. This is **unverified**: the page was not fetched, and its numbers differ from the release count above.

10. **Claim:** Steam games collectively generated $16.2B from January to mid-November 2025, a record, and Valve earned more than $4B in commission.
    Source: https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/11/14/analytics-in-the-incomplete-year-of-2025-games-on-steam-have-earned-over-16-billion-a-record. Publisher: Game World Observer (Alinea Analytics data). Pub: 2025-11-14. Confidence: medium. Class: A.

## Gaps
- No verified mobile vs PC revenue split or total gamer count from Niko or Newzoo. Market-size sources conflict ($430M vs $825M mobile-only vs $1.66B).
- Not established: whether an individual dev selling a premium game only via Steam (a foreign platform) legally needs a G4 certificate.
- No primary source on taxes or payouts for Vietnamese Steam earners.
- Console regulation and console market for Vietnam: not researched.
- Competitions, incubators and funds: only the draft incentives were found; nothing concrete on grants or competitions.
