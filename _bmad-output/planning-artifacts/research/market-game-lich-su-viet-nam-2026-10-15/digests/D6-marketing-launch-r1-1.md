# D6 — Marketing, wishlists, launch & funding (round 1, digest 1)

Decision served: beginner solo/small-team dev marketing & releasing a Vietnamese-history game on Steam.
Accessed (all): 2026-10-15. Classes: P = primary/first-party data, S = secondary report of primary data, A = aggregator/vendor blog (lower trust).

## Findings

1. **Claim:** Wishlist-to-first-week-sales median is 0.15x for games launching with 25k+ wishlists (Sep 2024–Sep 2025); 0.10x for games priced >$10; ~0.14x excl. NSFW. Ratios vary by an order of magnitude between games. Underperformers averaged 67% review score vs 91% for outperformers, and spent 411 vs 214 days in pre-release (Coming Soon).
   - Source: https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamediscoverco-the-state-of-steam — GameDev Reports summarizing GameDiscoverCo — 2025-11-03 — Confidence: medium-high — Class: S
   - Caveat: sample is 25k+ wishlist games; small games may convert differently.

2. **Claim:** Only 9% (141 of 1,500) of games sold more copies in first month than their launch wishlist count; wishlists explain ~49% (R²) of first-month sales variance; prediction weaker below 100k wishlists. 66% of games had <10k wishlists; only 6% (301) >100k.
   - Source: https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/video-game-insights-steam-wishlists — GameDev Reports summarizing Video Game Insights — 2025-07-23 — Confidence: medium — Class: S

3. **Claim:** In June 2026 Valve raised the "Popular Upcoming" threshold from ~7,000 wishlists to ~100,000 (Valve: "to better capture the most anticipated releases"). Implication: the widely-quoted "7k wishlists → Popular Upcoming" rule is obsolete; Steam Personal Calendar now matters for launch visibility.
   - Source: https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/06/25/ — How To Market A Game (Chris Zukowski) — 2026-06-25 — Confidence: medium (excerpt only fetched; Valve primary announcement not retrieved) — Class: S

4. **Claim:** Steam Next Fest rules: a title may participate in only ONE Next Fest; must be unreleased through the event; must have a public playable demo by start; store page must be published/public before registration. Upcoming editions: Oct 2026, Feb 2027, Jun 2027.
   - Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest — Valve Steamworks docs — undated living doc — Confidence: high — Class: P

5. **Claim:** Next Fest amplifies existing momentum rather than creating it: Feb 2026 had 3,500+ demos; games entering with <1,000 wishlists rarely reached higher tiers; top-tier games almost always entered with 10k+. Reported median gain conflicts across sources (~460 per Cinevva/presskit.gg; ~806 per another summary); strong correlation with pre-fest wishlists (Spearman ~0.825, unverified secondary claim).
   - Source: https://app.cinevva.com/news/2026-03-05-steam-next-fest-february-2026 — Cinevva (citing presskit.gg) — 2026-03-05 — Confidence: low-medium — Class: A

6. **Claim:** June 2026 Next Fest was the largest ever (~4,382 demos tracked, +66% YoY); "true median" game earned ~200 wishlists; only 788 games entered with >2,000 wishlists.
   - Source: https://recognizingpatterns.substack.com/p/steam-next-fest-june-2026-communities (search snippet; not fetched) — independent Substack — 2026 — Confidence: low — Class: A

7. **Claim:** Wishlist sources reported by Polden Publishing across its titles: vertical-video influencers (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) 35%; Steam algorithm 25%; friends of wishlisters 25%; Twitch/YouTube streamers 10%; developer's own posts (Reddit/social) 5%. Vertical video works best around announcements, demo launches, playtests.
   - Source: https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-51014 — WN Hub (reporting Polden Publishing) — 2026-06-02 — Confidence: medium (single publisher's portfolio) — Class: S

8. **Claim:** Median price of top-50 non-F2P Steam sellers (by units) fell from $19.50 (Feb 2023) to $15.64 (Oct 2025), −20%; revenue-weighted $23.70 → $20.35. Growth in $5–15 indie bracket; $25–55 "middle market" hard for new IP.
   - Source: https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/are-steam-game-prices-dropping-and — GameDiscoverCo (Simon Carless) — 2025-11-21 — Confidence: high — Class: P

9. **Claim:** Valve's 2023 regional-pricing recommendation update raised Vietnam's recommended prices ~47% (e.g., a $59.99 game: 480,000 → 705,000 VND). Recommendations are optional; developers set their own regional prices. Current VND tier table not retrieved.
   - Source: https://gearvn.com/blogs/tin-game/steam-nang-gia-ban-khuyen-nghi-cua-game-viet-nam-tang-47-argentina-len-toi-485 — GearVN — 2023-08-27 — Confidence: medium — Class: S (stale >12 months)

10. **Claim:** Kickstarter video-game campaigns: 32% success rate overall (Feb best 35%, Dec worst 28%); 2024 total $25.2M across ~1,335 campaigns; 81.8% of funds came from goals ≤$500K; none of 15 campaigns seeking $2.5M+ succeeded. (Search snippet also cites 2025: 443 successful campaigns, ~$26M — not verified in fetched text.)
    - Source: https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/kickstarter-in-2025-and-before — GameDev Reports (analysis by Dmitrii Filatov, Narwhal.ax, 17k campaigns since 2009) — 2025-11-06 — Confidence: medium — Class: S

11. **Claim:** Toge Productions (Indonesia; Coffee Talk, A Space for the Unbound) publishes SEA developers' games and runs the Toge Game Fund Initiative: up to US$10,000 no-strings prototype funding + mentorship; Vietnam eligible; developer keeps IP; Toge gets right of first refusal on publishing. (2021 terms — current status unverified.)
    - Source: https://www.siliconera.com/coffee-talk-developer-toge-productions-opens-fund-for-southeast-asian-indie-games/ — Siliconera — 2021-06-08 — Confidence: medium (stale) — Class: S

12. **Claim:** Vietnam government action plan for Resolutions 79–80 targets training 5,000 specialists to develop educational video games on history, a "each heritage site – one story – one digital product" model, a PPP cultural & arts fund, and Vietnam Talent Programme 2026–2035. Separately, proposed tax incentives (2-yr CIT exemption + 4-yr 50% reduction) and a formal "Vietnamese game" legal concept (search snippet, VnEconomy 2026-07-27, not fetched).
    - Source: https://vietnamnews.vn/life-style/1766688/5-000-game-developers-and-digital-heritage-key-targets-of-resolutions-79-80.html — Viet Nam News — 2026-03-04 — Confidence: medium — Class: S (gov't plan reported by state media; no concrete grant application route found)

## Practical synthesis (inference from above, not separately sourced)
- Treat wishlists as necessary but not sufficient; plan for ~0.1–0.15x week-one ratio (F1) and quality/review score as a key driver.
- Do the one Next Fest only once a demo is strong and pre-fest wishlists are built (F4–F5).
- Price likely in $5–15 band for a small first game (F8); review VND regional price manually (F9).
- Vertical short video + streamers likely the main external levers (F7).
- Vietnam history theme may align with new state cultural-industry policy (F12) — a possible funding/PR angle, needs verification.
