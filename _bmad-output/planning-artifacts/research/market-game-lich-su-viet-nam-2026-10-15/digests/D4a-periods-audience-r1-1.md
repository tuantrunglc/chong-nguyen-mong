# D4a — Historical periods & audience appeal (round 1, digest 1)
Accessed: 2026-10-15 for all sources. Method: WebSearch/WebFetch only. Most figures below come from search-result snippets; I fetched the full page only where marked [fetched]. Classes: FACT (number or event in the source), SIGNAL (indirect evidence about demand), INFERENCE (my reasoning, marked as such).

## Findings

1. **Claim:** "Mưa đỏ" (2025), about the 81-day defence of the Quảng Trị citadel in 1972, passed 701 billion VND on 26 Sept 2025 and finished around 714 billion VND. That makes it the highest-grossing Vietnamese film ever, and it set a one-day record of 55 billion VND on 1 Sept, beating "Mai" at 43.9 billion VND.
   Source: https://baovanhoa.vn/nghe-thuat/mua-do-vuot-700-ti-dong-sap-roi-rap-sau-con-sot-phong-ve-170405.html ; https://baolaocai.vn/phim-mua-do-vuot-moc-doanh-thu-465-ty-dong-thiet-lap-hang-loat-ky-luc-post881312.html — Báo Văn Hóa / Báo Lào Cai, around Sept 2025 (exact date not captured). Confidence: High (two outlets agree). Class: FACT.
   Note: this shows demand for **modern war (1972)** stories with state backing and a patriotic release window. It is not direct evidence for pre-modern periods.

2. **Claim:** "Địa đạo: Mặt trời trong bóng tối" (2025, director Bùi Thạc Chuyên, about the Củ Chi/Bình An Đông tunnels) took more than 170 billion VND, with one source giving 172.5 billion VND. The press calls this "rare" for a revolutionary war film. It also competed at the Tokyo International Film Festival 2025.
   Source: https://plo.vn/sap-cham-moc-160-ti-phim-dia-dao-cua-bui-thac-chuyen-ra-mat-phien-ban-moi-post846545.html (Pháp Luật TP.HCM, 2025); https://vietnamnews.vn/life-style/1726461/vietnamese-filmmaker-digs-deep-at-tokyo-film-festival.html (Việt Nam News, 2025). Confidence: Medium-High. Class: FACT.

3. **Claim:** "Đào, Phở và Piano" (2024), a state-commissioned film about the Hà Nội battle of winter 1946–47, made more than 20 billion VND while it was only showing at the National Cinema Center. Young people queued for tickets and online ticket systems were overloaded, and the film then went into wide release from 22 Feb 2024.
   Source: https://tuoitre.vn/tin-tuc-giai-tri-22-2-dao-pho-va-piano-se-chieu-rong-ra-ca-nuoc-20240222152858729.htm (Tuổi Trẻ, 22 Feb 2024); https://baovanhoa.vn/dien-anh/chat-rap-vi-dao-pho-va-piano-73044.html (Báo Văn Hóa, Feb 2024). Confidence: Medium (the revenue figure is from a snippet). Class: FACT + SIGNAL (young audiences have an appetite for historical/patriotic stories).

4. **Claim:** "Long Thần Tướng" is a comic set in the Trần dynasty during the second Mongol invasion. It won Silver at the 9th Japan International Manga Award, chosen from 259 entries from 46 countries and territories, and has been translated into English and Spanish. Its 2014 crowdfunding campaign, Vietnam's first for comic publishing, raised about 150 million of its 300 million VND target (US$14,280) in two weeks. A film adaptation was announced, with Ngô Thanh Vân producing.
   Source: https://ovietnam.vietnamnews.vn/readers-fund-comic-series-comeback-post254671.html (Việt Nam News, 10 May 2014) [fetched]; https://vietnam.vnanet.vn/english/vietnamese-artists-receive-silver-at-japans-international-manga-award/222752.html (VNA, date n/c); https://saigoneer.com/film-tv/19626-historical-comic-series-long-thần-tướng-to-be-adapted-into-movie-in-2023 (Saigoneer, date n/c). Confidence: Medium-High. Class: FACT + SIGNAL. The **Trần dynasty vs Mongols** setting already has a fan base and an international award, so it has been tested on readers both at home and abroad.

5. **Claim:** Civilization VI added Vietnam as a civilization with Bà Triệu (3rd century) as its leader, sold together with Kublai Khan in the New Frontier Pass DLC. Its design is built around defending terrain: "Drive Out The Aggressors" gives bonuses in woods, rainforest and marsh, and the Voi Chiến is a war-elephant unit.
   Source: https://civilization.2k.com/es-MX/civ-vi/new-frontier-pass/vietnam-kublai-khan/ (2K, 2020); https://primagames.com/?p=314637 (Prima Games, 2020). Confidence: High that it exists; reception is **unverified** (I have no review or sales data). Class: FACT. INFERENCE: a global AAA strategy game chose a pre-modern Vietnamese figure, and its theme of resisting invaders on home terrain lines up with the Trưng/Triệu → Trần story arc.

6. **Claim:** Academic studies of Vietnam War games (Call of Duty: Black Ops and about 10 others) find they take an "American perspective only" and borrow from Hollywood films. Vietnamese characters are mostly absent or appear only as enemies.
   Source: https://www.ceeol.com/search/article-detail?id=1169265 (CEEOL-indexed journal article, date n/c); https://muni.cz/en/research/publications/1130035 (Masaryk University, date n/c). Confidence: Medium. Class: FACT (scholarly) + SIGNAL: international players mostly know Vietnam through the American war, so pre-modern Vietnam is close to a blank slate for them. That is both an opportunity (novelty) and a cost (players need more context).

7. **Claim:** Ghost of Tsushima, set during the Mongol invasion of Japan, had sold more than 13 million copies by Sept 2024. The PC version sold about 800,000 in its first 20 days after launching on 16 May 2024.
   Source: https://destructoid.com/?p=606647 (Destructoid, 2024, citing Sucker Punch via the NYT); https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/video-game-insights-how-much-money (GameDev Reports, 2024). Confidence: High. Class: FACT. INFERENCE: players around the world accept "resisting the Mongol invasion" as a theme, which makes the Trần–Mongol wars (1258/1285/1288) easy to pitch to a foreign audience in one line. Comparing a small indie game to an AAA title, however, says very little about how many copies an indie game would sell.

8. **Claim:** Black Myth: Wukong sold more than 25 million copies, with 10 million in its first 3 days. Niko Partners (Daniel Ahmad) estimates only about 25% of sales came from outside China (as of Sept 2024).
   Source: https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/01/31/black-myth-wukong-25m-copies-sold-merchandise-china (Game World Observer, 31 Jan 2025) [fetched]. Confidence: High. Class: FACT. Lesson (INFERENCE): even a blockbuster built on a nation's culture sold mainly at home, so a Vietnamese historical game should plan for **Vietnamese players to be the core audience** and treat international sales as extra.

9. **Claim:** Raji: An Ancient Epic, an action game drawing on Indian mythology from small Pune studio Nodding Heads, had 82% positive reviews out of 2,859 on Steam. It won awards such as "Best of MIX Live", and reviewers compared it to God of War and Prince of Persia.
   Source: https://notebookcheck.net/loved-by-82-of-players-this-action-adventure-game-is-back-at-its-lowest-price-on-steam.1413315.0.html (Notebookcheck, 2025, date n/c); https://gameluster.com/raji-an-ancient-epic-brings-indian-mythology-history-to-nintendo-switch/ (Gameluster, 2020). Confidence: Medium. Class: FACT. INFERENCE: for a small team, **myth plus familiar gameplay** is a workable formula. 2,859 reviews points to a modest number of sales (sales figures not verified).

10. **Claim:** Vietnam bans foreign media that shows the nine-dash line. It banned the film "Uncharted" in 2022 and "Abominable" in 2019, and in 2021 required Netflix to remove episodes of "Pine Gap".
   Source: https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/vietnam-bans-uncharted-1235110778/ (The Hollywood Reporter, Mar 2022); https://english.alarabiya.net/News/world/2022/03/12/Vietnam-blocks-Sony-s-Uncharted-movie-starring-Tom-Holland-over-South-China-Sea-map (Al Arabiya, 12 Mar 2022). Confidence: High. Class: FACT. Any game that includes **maps or territory** must follow Vietnam's official position (Hoàng Sa/Trường Sa), and that position may clash with China's market rules (China-side rules not researched in this digest).

11. **Claim:** Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP, effective 25 Dec 2024, says a game can only be licensed if its content does not breach the Cybersecurity Law or IP law, and must not contain terrorism, violence, obscenity, or content that offends the nation's cultural traditions. It also limits players under 18 to 60 minutes per game per day (180 minutes across all games).
   Source: https://vietnamnet.vn/tuyen-bai/nghi-dinh-147-2024-nd-cp-0000YD.html (VietNamNet, 2024–25); https://danchuphapluat.vn/tu-ngay-25122024-nguoi-duoi-18-tuoi-khong-duoc-choi-game-qua-60-phuttro-choi-2444.html. Confidence: Medium (summarised from snippets). Class: FACT. **Gap:** I did not confirm the exact wording of the ban on "distorting history" for games. The Cybersecurity Law (Art. 16) is believed to prohibit distorting history, but that is **unverified belief**.

12. **Claim:** Historical films and dramas in Vietnam have repeatedly drawn criticism for historical inaccuracy, especially costumes. The Nguyễn-court drama "Phượng Khấu" was watched closely and hired historian Lê Văn Lan as an advisor.
   Source: https://baophapluat.vn/phim-co-trang-viet-thanh-cong-den-tu-nhung-co-phuc-dung-lich-su-post422377.html (Báo Pháp Luật, date n/c); https://nhandan.vn/ocop/gian-nan-lam-phim-lich-su-post731001.html (Nhân Dân, date n/c). Confidence: Medium. Class: SIGNAL. Vietnamese audiences police authenticity, especially costumes; a historical advisor reduces the risk.

13. **Claim:** Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư was compiled by Ngô Sĩ Liên in 1479, and the 1697 edition is the one that survives. The standard Vietnamese translation is by Ngô Đức Thọ et al. (1983–). Lĩnh Nam chích quái is a collection of myths from the Trần period and influenced later official histories. A copy of the original text is in the Nôm Foundation library.
   Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Đại_Việt_sử_ký_toàn_thư (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10-15); https://science.thanglong.edu.vn/index.php/vola/article/view/346 (Thăng Long University journal, date n/c); https://lib.nomfoundation.org/collection/1/volume/962. Confidence: Medium. Class: FACT. INFERENCE: the original Hán-text works are centuries old and almost certainly public domain, but **modern translations (e.g. 1983) may still be under copyright**. Copyright status was not verified.
