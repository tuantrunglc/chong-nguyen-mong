# D2: Competitive landscape, Vietnamese history/folklore games (round 1, digest 1)
Accessed: 2026-10-15 for all sources. Classes: FACT (primary/reputable report), ESTIMATE (third-party model like SteamSpy/steamdata), CLAIM (press/secondary repeating studio statements), LOW (weak or aggregator source).

## Findings

1. **Thần Trùng / The Death (DUT Studio)**: Vietnamese psychological horror set in a Hanoi rented house, 2–4 h playtime, 3 endings. Made by a 3-person indie studio. Price $5.99. 2,890 Steam reviews, 91.32% positive ("Very Positive"). Estimated 54.17k units, about $248k gross, 50.54k wishlists.
   - Source: https://steamdata.ai/game/1726400, steamdata.ai, page undated
   - Plot/endings detail: https://gearvn.com/blogs/tin-game/than-trung-tua-game-kinh-di-viet-nam-mang-theo-hoai-bao-cua-dung-ct-chinh-thuc-ra-mat, GearVN, 2022
   - Confidence: medium (units and revenue are model estimates). Class: ESTIMATE + FACT (review count).
   - Release date conflict: search snippet says 25 Aug 2022, steamdata says 15 Sep 2022. Unresolved.

2. **The Scourge / Tai Ương (Rare Reversee + Beaztek)**: first-person horror puzzle game drawn from 1990s Saigon urban legends. Full release 28 Mar 2026 after Early Access. Price $19.99. 5 Asian languages plus English. China distribution through LICOGAME. SteamSpy: 93% user score, 50k–100k owners, 17 peak concurrent players "yesterday".
   - Source: https://steamspy.com/app/2456350, SteamSpy, page undated
   - A search snippet (secondary) cited 787 reviews, about 51,769 copies and about $1.03M revenue: https://gamegeeker.com/games/the-scourge and https://vaporlens.app/app/2456350/the_scourge_tai_ương/stats/details. Not opened directly.
   - Summer sale 20% was "deepest discount to date": https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/the-scourge-tai-ng-20-off-for-steam-summer-sale, Game Developer (press release), 2026
   - Confidence: medium (owner band) / low (revenue figure). Class: ESTIMATE.

3. **7554 (Emobi Games, 2011)**: offline first-person shooter set in the 1946–54 anti-French war, ending at Điện Biên Phủ. Described as the first Vietnamese PC game in modern 3D.
   - Source: https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Companies/Emobigames, Giant Bomb, undated (search snippet)
   - Tuổi Trẻ (10 Feb 2012) called it a "tiền tỉ" (billion-VND) project that drew WSJ coverage. No sales figures given: https://tuoitre.vn/emobi.html
   - Confidence: high on existence and genre, NONE on sales. Class: FACT / CLAIM.
   - Studio name conflict: some VN sources credit it to "Hiker Games" (later rebrand?). Unverified.

4. **300475 (Hiker Games)**: FPS recreating the 1975 Hồ Chí Minh Campaign, announced May 2021. A search snippet cites a 40-person team, 30 months and a 20-billion-VND budget.
   - Sources: https://bbwin.it.com/chuyen-muc/gamez/hiker-games-va-nhung-tua-game-danh-dau-cot-moc-lich-su-lang-game-viet.html (aggregator, 11 May 2021); https://cellphones.com.vn/sforum/game-viet-300475-tai-hien-chien-thang-lich-su-giai-phong-mien-nam-chinh-thuc-lo-dien-teaser-dau-tien (CellphoneS Sforum)
   - Release status and outcome were NOT found.
   - Confidence: low. Class: LOW/CLAIM.

5. **Toy Odyssey (Hiker Games, 2016)**: called the "first purely Vietnamese game on Steam". Reportedly reached the top 8 hottest on Steam in week 1. This is not a history game.
   - Source: same bbwin aggregator (2021)
   - Confidence: low. Class: CLAIM.

6. **Sử Hộ Vương (Phạm Vĩnh Lộc, Hồ Phương Thảo; 2019)**: a physical card game built on Vietnamese history in anime-style 2D art. Comicola crowdfunding raised over 300M VND from about 800 backers. Sold 6,000 units in the first 10 days.
   - Strong backlash over sexualized historical figures (Hồ Xuân Hương), flippant handling of the Nguyễn Huệ / Nguyễn Ánh conflict, Japanese terms and historical inaccuracy, peaking after a Shark Tank VN appearance.
   - Source: https://gearvn.com/blogs/cong-nghe/su-ho-vuong-dang-khen-va-dang-trach, GearVN, 27 Aug 2023
   - Confidence: medium. Class: CLAIM.
   - Lesson: a history-based IP can raise money and sell, but tone and accuracy draw strong public scrutiny.

7. **Thuận Thiên Kiếm**: billed as the first online game based on Vietnamese "dã sử" (semi-legendary history), with over 1,000 culture/history quests. It is an older MMO.
   - Source: https://vietnamnet.vn/thuan-thien-kiem-chuan-bi-xuat-chinh-i315614, VietNamNet, date not captured
   - Outcome not found. Confidence: low. Class: CLAIM.

9. **Pattern (inference, not a sourced fact)**: the Vietnamese-made titles with verifiable Steam traction (Thần Trùng, Tai Ương) are both short first-person horror games set in recent urban Vietnam (1990s). They are not dynastic-history games. The dynastic/war history projects (7554, 300475, Thuận Thiên Kiếm) have no retrievable commercial data.
   - Class: INFERENCE from findings 1–7.

10. **Steam access in Vietnam**:
    - May 2024: VN ISPs (Viettel, VNPT) blocked Steam via DNS. The official reason given was that games distributed in VN need local approval and Valve did not respond to authorities (Director General Lê Quang Tự Do).
    - Oct 2024: reports that the block extended to downloads, updates and cloud.
    - Source: https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26798/steam-effectively-blocked-in-vietnam-issues-extend-to-downloads-and-updates, Inven Global, Oct 2024
    - Status as of 2026 is unknown.
    - Confidence: medium. Class: FACT (news report).
    - Implication: the domestic Steam audience is friction-laden, so a VN dev should plan for a global/English Steam audience or a licensed domestic channel.

11. **Policy**:
    - A draft decree amending Decree 147/2024/NĐ-CP (Broadcasting/Electronic Information Dept, now under MCST) aims to promote "game products bearing distinctive features of Vietnamese culture and history", citing Resolution 80-NQ/TW and Resolution 28/2026/QH16.
    - Separately, tax incentives for "game Việt" have been proposed.
    - Sources: https://daibieunhandan.vn/tiep-suc-cho-nganh-game-viet-10429971.html (Đại Biểu Nhân Dân, 2026, exact date not captured); https://vneconomy.vn/de-xuat-loat-uu-dai-thue-chua-tung-co-cho-game-viet.htm (VnEconomy, date not captured)
    - Confidence: medium (draft, not enacted). Class: FACT (draft policy).

12. **Market context**:
    - Vietnamese-studio games earned about $315M in 2024, projected to exceed $430M in 2025 (+36.4%). The market is mostly mobile.
    - Source: https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/vietnams-gaming-revenue-on-track-to-exceed-430-mln-in-2025.htm, VnEconomy, 28 Jun 2025
    - About 19,200 Steam releases in 2025; 2,200 had zero reviews and 7,100 had at most 9.
    - Source: https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-49561, WN Hub, 2026
    - Confidence: medium. Class: FACT/ESTIMATE.
