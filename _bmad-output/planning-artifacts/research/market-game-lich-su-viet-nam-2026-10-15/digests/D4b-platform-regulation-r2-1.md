## Findings

1. **Claim:** Only Vietnamese enterprises can apply for G1–G4 game licences; G2/G3/G4 require a certificate of registration of game service provision. Individuals cannot hold one directly.
   - Source: https://viettonkinconsulting.com/business-incorporation/how-to-start-a-gaming-company-in-vietnam-a-6-step-guideline/ | Viettonkin Consulting | pub date not shown in retrieved text | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: medium (consultancy, consistent with R1 Decree 147 Art.37) | class: REG

2. **Claim:** Games sold on Steam reach Vietnamese players without Vietnamese regulator pre-approval; developers only submit to Steam. This gap is the reported context for complaints and Steam blocking in Vietnam.
   - Source: same Viettonkin search result set plus https://gamedeveloper.com/business/steam-suddenly-banned-in-vietnam | Game Developer | pub date not retrieved | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: low-medium (snippet only; not fetched) | class: REG

3. **Claim:** No retrieved source (law firm, thuvienphapluat Q&A or developer interview) says outright whether a Vietnamese individual selling *only to foreign players* via Steam/App Store/Google Play needs a G4 certificate. Thần Trùng (DUT Studio) launched on Steam on 15 Sep 2022 at 75,000 VND with Vietnamese voice-over. The retrieved coverage does not say how its licensing was handled.
   - Source: https://gearvn.com/blogs/tin-game/than-trung-tua-game-kinh-di-viet-nam-mang-theo-hoai-bao-cua-dung-ct-chinh-thuc-ra-mat/ | GearVN | ~Sep 2022 | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: high for the launch facts; licensing = GAP | class: CASE

4. **Claim:** Game management sits with the Cục Phát thanh, truyền hình và thông tin điện tử (Authority of Broadcasting and Electronic Information, ABEI), which is now under the **Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism (MCST / Bộ VHTTDL)**. On 10 Jul 2025 it revoked 27 G1 game approvals.
   - Source: https://baovanhoa.vn/bao-chi/cuc-phat-thanh-truyen-hinh-va-thong-tin-dien-tu-thu-hoi-27-game-g1-157794.html | Báo Văn hóa (MCST newspaper) | 2025-07-30 | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: high | class: INST

5. **Claim:** Decree 43/2025/NĐ-CP (28 Feb 2025) sets MCST's functions, which now include "thông tin điện tử" (electronic information), and lists ABEI as one of MCST's units (item 18). It does not name games explicitly. The game role is inferred from ABEI's move to MCST plus finding 4.
   - Source: https://vov.vn/chinh-tri/nhiem-vu-co-cau-to-chuc-cua-bo-van-hoa-the-thao-va-du-lich-post1158106.vov | VOV | 2025-03-01 | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: high (structure); medium (game-role inference) | class: INST

6. **Claim:** Individuals earning from app platforms (Google/Apple) owe tax on the full amount received. For software services the rate is 0% VAT and 2% PIT on revenue. For in-app advertising or digital content services it is 5% VAT + 5% PIT. Cited bases: VAT Law 48/2024/QH15 and tax-authority letters 1063/CT-CS (12 Feb 2026) and 2965/CT-CS (12 May 2026) on software classification.
   - Source: https://gonnapass.com/viet-app-qua-nen-tang-chiu-thue-nao-shopify-google-apple-store/ | Gonnapass (accounting/tax blog) | 2026-10-05 | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: medium (secondary blog; the official letters were not fetched) | class: TAX

7. **Claim:** Decree 141/2026/NĐ-CP (29 Apr 2026) amends Decree 68/2026 and raises the revenue threshold for household and individual businesses from 500 million to **1 billion VND/year**, applied from 1 Jan 2026. At or below this threshold, no VAT and no PIT are due. Above it, e-invoices are mandatory. Presumptive tax (thuế khoán) is being replaced by declaration from 2026.
   - Source: https://tapchicongthuong.vn/chinh-thuc--nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-1-ty-dong-nam--ap-dung-tu-1-1-2026-514336.htm and https://www.sggp.org.vn/nang-nguong-chiu-thue-voi-ho-kinh-doanh-len-1-ty-dongnam-post850439.html | Tạp chí Công Thương; SGGP | 2026 (Apr–May; exact day not retrieved) | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: medium-high (several outlets agree in search snippets; decree text not fetched) | class: TAX

8. **Claim:** Unverified scale signal: in 2022, individuals' detected income from foreign sources such as Google/Apple totalled 928 billion VND. One Hanoi individual earned 330 billion VND from apps and paid 23.4 billion VND in tax (2020 data).
   - Source: https://plo.vn/ca-nhan-thu-nhap-khung-tu-google-apple-len-toi-928-ti-dongnam-post715769.html ; https://vnbusiness.vn/thue-ngan-sach/viet-phan-mem-dang-tai-google-play-va-app-store-mot-ca-nhan-nop-thue-hon-23-ty-dong-1076276.html | PLO; VnBusiness | 2021–2023 (not fetched) | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: low-medium (snippet only) | class: TAX

9. **Claim:** Steamworks: when a partner's country has no income tax treaty with the US, Valve must withhold **30%** of the payment and remit it to the IRS. The withholding applies only to the "US Share", meaning revenue from US customers.
   - Source: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/taxfaq | Valve (Steamworks docs) | undated living doc | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: high | class: TAX

10. **Claim:** No US–Vietnam income tax treaty is in force. The treaty signed in 2015 has not been ratified by the US Senate, so Part II (treaty claim) of the W-8BEN stays blank for Vietnamese residents. Result: 30% withholding on the US share of Steam revenue.
   - Source: https://www.omnivoo.com/blog/paying-vietnamese-contractors-from-us-company (and DFDL tax alert https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/tax-alert-the-income-tax-treaty-between-vietnam) | Omnivoo; DFDL | 2026 / 2015 (not fetched) | accessed 2026-10-15 | confidence: medium (snippets; IRS treaty page not checked this run) | class: TAX

No prize or fund amounts were found.
