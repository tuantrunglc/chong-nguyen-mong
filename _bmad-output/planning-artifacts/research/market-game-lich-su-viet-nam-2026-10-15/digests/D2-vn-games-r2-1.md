# D2 — Vietnamese/SEA history & culture games — Round 2 digest

Accessed: 2026-10-15 for all sources. Tool calls: 18. Sources used: 9.
Class key: F = fact reported by source; E = third-party estimate; A = analyst inference (mine).

## Findings

1. **Claim:** Steam access in Vietnam was restricted again from 2–3 Oct 2026, and the scope is wider than before: users report problems with the store, downloads/updates, community features and Cloud sync. It varies by ISP and region (some FPT users affected, others not). A VPN works; changing DNS did not for some users. There has been no formal government announcement about the October restrictions.
   - Source: https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26798/steam-effectively-blocked-in-vietnam-issues-extend-to-downloads-and-updates — Inven Global — pub 2026-10-03 — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: Medium (one trade outlet, based on community reports) — Class: F
2. **Claim:** Vietnamese authorities officially confirmed blocking some Steam services in May 2024. An official (Le Quang Tu Do, ABEI/MIC) said games distributed in Vietnam need local approval and Valve had not responded. Decree 147/2024/ND-CP, in force since 25 Dec 2024, requires games to be licensed.
   - Source: same Inven Global article (pub 2026-10-03) — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: Medium-High — Class: F
3. **Implication (A):** For a team based in Vietnam, the Steam block mainly hurts **domestic** reach (Vietnamese players without a VPN) and the team's own dev workflow (Steamworks/uploads may need a VPN). It does not affect global sales. A history game aimed at Vietnamese players should plan a second channel (mobile/itch/web), or accept VPN friction. Unverified: whether Steamworks partner uploads are blocked.
   - Class: A
4. **Claim:** The Scourge | Tai Ương — developers: Rare Reversee and Beaztek. Publishers: Rare Reversee (global) and LICOGAME (China). Early Access 2024-10-23, full release 2026-03-28. Price 350,000₫. Steam reviews: Very Positive, 93% of 1,207 (all-time). Setting: 1990s Saigon, first-person horror puzzle. Note: Round 1 named the studio "Teabreak"; the Steam page does not support that.
   - Source: https://store.steampowered.com/app/2456350/ — Valve/Steam — page live — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: High — Class: F
5. **Claim:** VaporLens estimates about 51,769 copies and about $1.03M gross revenue for Tai Ương, assuming $20 per copy (787 reviews, data as of 2026-03-29). The estimate looks inflated: the review-ratio method and a $20 price above regional pricing both push it up. Its 73.5% positive figure also conflicts with Steam's 93%. Treat it as an upper bound.
   - Source: https://vaporlens.app/app/2456350/the_scourge_tai_ương/stats/details — VaporLens — updated 2026-03-29 — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: Low — Class: E
6. **Claim:** Nine Sols (Red Candle Games, Taiwan; Taoism/East Asian-myth themed "Taopunk") sold more than 800k units across all platforms about a year after its March 2024 PC launch.
   - Source: https://gonintendo.com/contents/49010-nine-sols-reaches-800k-sold — GoNintendo — pub 2025-05-29 — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: High (developer announcement) — Class: F
7. **Claim:** Red Candle Games has about 12 employees (another figure cited is 16 at one point during Nine Sols). Detention (2017, Taiwan White Terror–era horror) has an estimated 500k–1M Steam owners according to SteamSpy.
   - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Candle_Games (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-10-15) and https://steamspy.com/dev/RedCandleGames (SteamSpy) — seen via search snippet only, not fetched
   - Confidence: Medium — Class: F (team) / E (owners)
8. **Claim:** A Space for the Unbound (Mojiken, Indonesia; set in late-1990s rural Indonesia) was released 2023-01-19 and built in Unity. The whole studio of about 12–14 people was moved onto it around 2019. Awards: Indonesia Game Awards 2023 GOTY; nominated for Games for Impact at TGA 2023. Wikipedia gives no sales figure.
   - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Space_for_the_Unbound — Wikipedia — accessed 2026-10-15
   - Confidence: Medium-High — Class: F
9. **Claim:** Coffee Talk (Toge Productions, Indonesia) was released 2020-01-29 and built in Unity. It earned about IDR 7.6B (~US$550k) gross in its first month. The core team was about 7 named people. A separate third-party estimate (SteamPulse) puts it at up to 369k Steam units, about $3.3M.
   - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_Talk_(video_game) — Wikipedia — accessed 2026-10-15. Estimate: https://steampulse.org/game/914800 (snippet only)
   - Confidence: Medium (revenue F) / Low (units E) — Class: F/E
10. **Claim:** Earlier Vietnamese games set in pre-modern history that turned up: *Nam Đế: Phục Hưng Đại Nghiệp* (iOS real-time strategy, Twelve Warlords period) and *Thuận Thiên Kiếm* (online RPG, feudal Northern Vietnam). *Vệ Thần* was only a concept project (Sơn Tinh, Thánh Gióng, Chử Đồng Tử). *Brother Hai's Phở Restaurant* (Hà Nội indie) mixes in history and myth. No Vietnamese-made game set in pre-modern history or myth with Steam release data for 2023–2026 was found in this run.
   - Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1451081334 (Apple App Store); https://vneconomy.vn/game-online-da-su-viet-nam-dau-tien-duoc-phat-hanh.htm (VnEconomy); https://saigoneer.com/saigon-technology/25542-... (Saigoneer) — snippets only, not fetched
   - Confidence: Low-Medium — Class: F (existence only)
11. **Implication (A):** The SEA/Asian comparables that succeeded (Detention, Nine Sols, A Space for the Unbound, Coffee Talk) were made by teams of about 7–16 people. They used narrow genres (2D horror adventure, 2D action, pixel adventure, visual novel) with strong local cultural flavour and aimed at the **global** Steam/console market, not mainly at home. Domestic Vietnamese buyers are not needed for a hit; international framing and polish are.
    - Class: A
